  1. Read the verdict: damage is pinned to leg 3, the last-mile carrier, with a 3.5 kg loss.
  2. Under Attack simulator, press Rewrite damage to intact. The verdict flips to tampered and names the broken signatures.
  3. Press Reset demo, then Delete a handover. The chain break is caught.
  4. Reset again, copy the ledger from Export, and paste it into Verify. Anyone can check it without trusting us.
  5. Record a new handover with a photo. Then use Check a photo with the same image to prove it matches the sealed fingerprint.
